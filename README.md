🏆 Majlis Arena – Family AI Game Day
A working web app for family games inspired by Emirati culture and sport. An AI judge watches and listens to real play (video + audio), applies the game rules and keeps the score.
Main Featuresa
3 games (Cycle 3): ⚽ Football Showdown (team) · ☕ Dallah Balance Walk (individual turns) · 🎙️ Lisan Twist (speech, individual turns)
Full 6-step flow: Choose game → Players & teams → Rules & ready → Round in play → AI judge results → Final results
Real AI judge: upload a video or record live with camera and microphone. The AI tracks players and the ball, spots goals, measures ball possession, finds the MVP, detects drops and listens to spoken answers.
Explained decisions: every result shows the key game moment and the reason for it.
Tamper-proof results: AI reports carry a SHA-256 code. The app rejects reports that were edited or did not come from our AI judge (also works for reports from a separate Python judge).
Family-friendly: timer, team scores, tie-break rules, MVP and a trophy shelf. No account, no server, no data leaves the device.
Run it (3 steps)
Extract the ZIP file.
Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (double-click it).
Test: pick a game → save the teams → start → in AI Judge upload/record a clip, or press 🧪 Demo for an instant simulated result → Apply AI results.
Internet is needed the first time (Tailwind and the AI vision model load from a CDN). Camera and microphone need your permission. Demo results are always labelled SIMULATED.
